Who we are

This website is run by Furlong Web3 Ltd, a company registered in England & Wales (Company No.13892172), registered office 57 Ely Street, Stratford-Upon-Avon. We are the controller of the personal data described here.

What we collect

When you use our contact form we receive the details you enter: your name, company, email address, the service you're interested in, and your message.

If you book a call, the booking is handled by Calendly, and Calendly's own privacy policy applies to the details you give them.

How we use it

We use your details only to respond to your enquiry and, if you become a client, to arrange the work you've asked for. We don't add you to a mailing list, sell your data, or share it with anyone for marketing. Our legal basis is our legitimate interest in replying to people who contact us.

Who processes it for us

  • Formspree delivers contact form messages to us. Formspree is based in the United States and transfers data under appropriate safeguards.
  • Our website host may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses) for security.
  • If enabled, we use privacy-friendly analytics that do not use cookies or identify individual visitors.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry emails for up to 24 months, unless they lead to a working relationship, in which case we keep them for as long as needed for that relationship and our legal obligations.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the personal data we hold about you, or object to how we use it. To do so, send us a message through the contact form and we'll reply within one month. You also have the right to complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

Cookies

This site does not set cookies. Our fonts are hosted on our own site, so no data is sent to third parties when the page loads.
