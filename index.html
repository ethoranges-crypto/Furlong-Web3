Onchain yield is the return earned by lending or supplying assets through blockchain-based financial applications. We help you decide whether, where and how to use it, with a risk based approach.

The challenge

Assets are moving onchain. Let us help you access them.

Customers want yield, be an early mover.

Blockchain allows users to access financial products faster, cheaper and more easily than traditional company offerings.

It's hard to differentiate real yield from risk

Headline rates can hide smart-contract, liquidity and counterparty risk. Navigating this constantly moving sector is challanging.

Generalist consultancies are slow & crypto teams don't speak your language

You need someone who understands both regulated finance and how DeFi protocols really work, and who can translate between the two.

About

Independent advice from inside DeFi

Furlong Web3 is a UK-based consultancy firm helping businesses create & grow their on-chain presence.

Founder

Simon Furlong has spent more than seven years working for on-chain businesses, as a founder and leading business development. He has supporting over 10 companies, delivering 20+ protocol integrations across lending, liquidity, vaults curation and staking. He has led partnerships, OTC and liquidity deals, and helped bring in more than $26m in capital and user deposits. Before crypto, he spent a decade in technology startups.
